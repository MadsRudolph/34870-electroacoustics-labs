\subsection*{Instructions}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0pt
\item Stay in your seat, keep your head still and face the loudspeaker. Please don't talk, react or look at the others' answers.
\item You will hear short music excerpts. You do \textbf{not} know which settings are used, and nobody in the room does either.
\item There are no right answers. Rate what \emph{you} hear. If you are unsure, make your best guess.
\item Each excerpt is played once. Ask the test leader if you need to hear it again.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{How the scales work}

\begin{itemize}\itemsep0pt
\item \textbf{Amount} (bass, treble), five boxes: left = far too little, middle = just right, right = far too much.
\item \textbf{1--5} scales, five boxes: left box = 1 = worst, right box = 5 = best. The words under each scale say which end is which.
\item \textbf{Overall, 0--10}: write a number. 10 = as good as you can imagine, 0 = as bad as you can imagine.
\end{itemize}
